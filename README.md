## Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 31, 2026**

Welcome to Robin Sound Labs. This Privacy Policy explains how our applications handle data on your Android device for both paid and ad-supported versions. You can find the full privacy policy text and structural details in the referenced web document.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
Our applications function as local, offline media players and do not collect or share personal identification information.

### 2. Device Permissions Used
Requests system permissions like storage access and foreground services solely to support local playback functionality.

### 3. Google Cast / Chromecast Integration & Local Network Operations
Allows local casting via an internal local HTTP server entirely within your private home network without external cloud processing.

### 4. Monetization & Advertising Framework
Paid versions contain no ads, while free ad-supported versions may utilize third-party ad providers like Google AdMob for ad delivery.
Paid version: Pure Audio Music Player,
Free ad-supported versions: Canary Player: Music Player, Fire Video Player.

### 5. Children's Privacy
Does not collect data from children under 13 and complies with family usage policies.
